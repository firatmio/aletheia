# Fonts

GFS Didot sets the Greek on the site. It is served from here as two unmodified Google Fonts WOFF2 subsets, `greek` and `greek-ext`.

It is licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org), copyright © Greek Font Society (https://www.greekfontsociety-gfs.gr).

Newsreader and IBM Plex come from the `@fontsource` packages instead; see `src/entry-client.tsx`.
