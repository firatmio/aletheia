# Code of conduct

Aletheia follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the issues, pull requests and discussions of this project, and wherever someone represents it.

In short:
- **Expected:** be kind and patient, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and assume good intent.
- **Not accepted:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language, and publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainer, Fırat Tuna Arslan, at **firattunaarslan@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer decides on the response, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
