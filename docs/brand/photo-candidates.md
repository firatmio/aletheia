**Licenses:**
- [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, no attribution required (credit is still nice).
- Unsplash+ photos are paid, so they are left out except one, marked below.

Map them to the asset numbers in `image-prompts.md`: 1/2 landing hero, 3 README banner, 4 how-it-works, 5 macro, 6 social card.

## Notes for choosing

- Many free anchor photos are heavily rusted or sit on beaches. The art direction wants clean steel and calm deep water, so the generated images will probably fit the dark hero better. Photos are likely stronger for chain and water textures and for the light still life.
- Anything we pick gets color-graded to the palette: navy, steel and parchment.
